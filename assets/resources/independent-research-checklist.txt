THE INDEPENDENT INVESTOR'S RESEARCH CHECKLIST
A companion worksheet from iStockLens

A source can be credible without every conclusion being right.
Use these questions with company filings and any sources you trust.
This worksheet does not verify claims for you.

1. STATE THE THESIS
[ ] What exactly do I believe about this business and its price?
[ ] Which parts are reported facts, estimates, model outputs or opinions?
[ ] What would need to be true for this investment to make sense?

2. CHECK THE SOURCE
[ ] Who produced the information, when, and using which assumptions?
[ ] Can I trace each material claim to a primary document?
[ ] Do figures use the same period, units and accounting definitions?
[ ] Are incentives, conflicts or missing context relevant?

3. QUESTION THE PRICE
[ ] What growth and profitability are already expected at this valuation?
[ ] Does cash generation support the reported earnings?
[ ] Am I confusing a strong company with an attractive investment?

4. LOOK FOR THE COUNTERCASE
[ ] What is the strongest reason my thesis could be wrong?
[ ] What information would make me wait, reconsider or walk away?
[ ] What remains uncertain or outside the model?

5. MAKE YOUR OWN CALL
[ ] Does this fit my financial situation, time horizon and risk tolerance?
[ ] Have I recorded my reasoning and the conditions for revisiting it?

Completing this checklist does not certify an investment as safe or suitable.
Research can improve a process; it cannot remove uncertainty or prevent losses.
iStockLens is a research tool, not a financial adviser. Verify independently.
