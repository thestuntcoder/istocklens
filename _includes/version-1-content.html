03 / A process, not a prediction

From “interesting”
to “I understand.”

For investors who do their homework.
Not for hot tips. Not for auto-trading.

  1. 01

    Pick a company.

    Start with a business you’re curious about. Open its report and get a structured view of what sits behind the ticker.

  2. 02

    Pressure-test the story.

    Compare quality with valuation. Look at the context. Ask where the numbers support the story—and where they don’t.

  3. 03

    Make your own call.

    Record your thesis. Keep an eye on your watchlist. Decide whether to look closer, wait, or walk away.

The goal isn’t more stock ideas. It’s a better reason for the ones you choose.
