01 / THE STANDARD

Trust a source.
Still check the claim.

A credible source deserves your attention. A conclusion deserves your scrutiny. That includes ours.

[ ? ]
A.

Separate the business from the price.

Growth, profitability and financial strength help you assess a company. Valuation asks a different question: what are you paying for those qualities?

In iStockLens: separate business-quality and valuation assessments.

B.

Look for the part that doesn’t fit.

A consensus is not a certainty. Consider price action, analyst expectations and news drivers—then look beyond the summary for missing context.

In iStockLens: price action, analyst consensus and targets, news and market themes.

C.

Make your reasoning reviewable.

Record why an idea makes sense, what remains uncertain, and what would change your mind. Give your future self something better than a memory.

In iStockLens: watchlists and a bullish, neutral or bearish thesis journal.

02 / CLEAR BOUNDARIES

A research tool.
Not an authority.

Useful analysis is clear about
where its usefulness ends.

WHAT ISTOCKLENS HELPS YOU EXAMINE

  • ↳ The company’s financial qualities
  • ↳ Valuation alongside business quality
  • ↳ Price action and wider market context
  • ↳ Your watchlist and recorded thesis

WHAT REMAINS YOURS TO VALIDATE

  • ↳ Material claims against primary sources
  • ↳ Assumptions, dates and missing context
  • ↳ Risks that a model may not capture
  • ↳ Fit with your finances and risk tolerance

* Ratings are quantitative. Summaries are automatically generated. Neither is a guarantee that a thesis is right.
